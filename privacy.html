Privacy Policy

Overview. Quiet CEO Publisher is a private automation tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own content — Reels and videos — to the owner's own Facebook Page "Quiet CEO" and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else logs in.

Data accessed. The tool accesses Page and Instagram account IDs, names, and basic profile info. It uses the permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, and business_management. Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's server.

What we do not do. We do not sell or share data, run ads, track visitors, or collect personal data from people who visit this page or watch the published content.

Retention and security. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to publish content. The owner can revoke access at any time.

Terms of Service

  • Use of Quiet CEO Publisher is limited to the owner of the connected Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • The tool is provided as is, with no warranty.
  • The owner may change or stop the service at any time.
  • Content published through the tool follows Meta's platform terms.

Data Deletion

To remove the app's access: On Facebook, go to Settings → Business Integrations and remove "Quiet CEO Publisher". For Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

To request deletion of any stored data: email hello@isoftro.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Data is deleted within 30 days.
